Bulk Packing (In Bales)

Our most popular option for high-volume importers. Industrial hydraulic presses compress wipers into dense bales — maximising container load capacity and significantly reducing shipping cost per kilogram. Bales are wrapped in heavy-duty woven polypropylene and secured with high-tensile strapping.

  • Sizes Available: 150lbs, 300lbs, 400lbs, 750lbs, 900lbs.
  • Protection: Waterproof HDPE outer layer.
  • Efficiency: Maximises 40HC container weight capacity.
  • Customization: Transparent or opaque wrapping options.
Request Quote for Bales

Palletized Packing

Ideal for distribution centres with forklift capabilities. Goods are stacked in standard cartons or compressed blocks onto wooden or plastic pallets. The entire load is shrink-wrapped and corner-guarded to ensure stability through ocean transit and inland trucking.

  • Standards: Euro Pallets or Standard Industrial Pallets.
  • Security: Heavy-gauge shrink wrap & corner posts.
  • Handling: 4-way entry for easy forklift access.
Request Pallet Rates

Retail Packing (Bags & Boxes)

Designed for direct-to-consumer sales or shelf display. We offer small-format packaging from 1lb to 50lbs — clear or branded poly bags, or heavy-duty dispenser boxes with a convenient pop-out hole. We can apply your custom box graphics, header cards, barcodes (UPC/EAN), and logos, making the product retail-ready on arrival.

  • Sizes: 1lbs, 2lbs, 5lbs, 10lbs, 25lbs and 50lbs.
  • Styles: Poly bags or heavy-duty cardboard dispenser boxes.
  • Branding: Custom printed header cards & labels.
  • Outer Pack: Retail bags packed into master cartons.

Shipping & Logistics

Freight Options to Suit Every Order

From full containers to urgent air shipments, we handle documentation and coordination to get your goods moving.

FCL — Full Container Load

The most cost-efficient option for high volumes. We maximise 20ft and 40HC containers with densely baled goods and full export documentation.

LCL — Less than Container

Consolidated shipping for smaller or trial orders. Pay only for the space you use while still receiving secure, export-grade packing.

Air Freight

For urgent samples and time-sensitive shipments, we arrange expedited air freight to keep your operations supplied.
